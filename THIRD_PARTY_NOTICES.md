## US state boundary data

The projected state geometry in `assets/mapdata.js` is derived from the
PublicaMundi “US states” GeoJSON dataset:

<https://github.com/PublicaMundi/MappingAPI>

The boundaries are derived from public-domain US Census Bureau/TIGER data.
The dataset is used here as public-domain geographic source material.

## Population and statehood facts

Estimated populations are approximate 2023 US Census Bureau estimates.
Order-of-statehood values follow the standard historical admission dates.
