How to assemble the atlas

I
Seat the states

Drag a colored state block from the left tray onto its faint silhouette. Get close — it snaps into place.

II
Turn it true north

Some blocks begin askew. While dragging, press R, double-click, or use the Rotate 45° button to turn the piece until it drops.

III
Crown the capitals

Drag a brass capital pin to the glowing socket of a placed state. A wrong pairing is rejected with a buzz.

IV
Inspect any placed state

Click a state on the map for total area, population, density and neighboring states. Alaska & Hawaii are on the Pacific inset.
